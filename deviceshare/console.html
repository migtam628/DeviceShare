Printer setup notes

Printers are discovered from the station's operating system and must also be explicitly approved under the Mac app's Local devices tab. A disabled queue cannot receive a DeviceShare test print.

If macOS shows “Printer is offline,” fix the physical connection or printer queue before sending more jobs. Do not repeatedly retry the same print.
